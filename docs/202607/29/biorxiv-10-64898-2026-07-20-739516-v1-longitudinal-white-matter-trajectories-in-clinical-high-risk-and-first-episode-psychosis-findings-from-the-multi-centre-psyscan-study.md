## 摘要
精神病与多个白质束弥散衍生分数各向异性(FA)的变化有关，然而大多数证据来自横断面研究。纵向研究结果不一致，白质随时间变化的临床相关性仍不清楚。为弥补这一空白，我们在大型多中心弥散张量成像研究中调查了早期精神病的纵向白质变化及其临床相关性。在18个中心，407名参与者（健康对照：n=97，59.8%男性，平均年龄23.9岁；临床高危精神病：n=158，53.5%男性，平均年龄23.0岁；首发精神病：n=152，71.1%男性，平均年龄25.1岁）在12个月内最多扫描了三次。评估了扣带束、上纵束、下额枕束以及全脑水平的FA。使用线性混合效应模型分析FA轨迹，以检验组别、社会和职业功能以及（减弱）精神病性症状的影响，同时控制人口统计学和社会经济协变量。未观察到组间FA有显著差异，无论是整体还是束内（p>0.05）。纵向FA轨迹与（减弱）精神病性症状或功能的变化无关（p>0.05）。相反，较高的基线抗精神病药物剂量与较低的FA显著相关（校正后p<0.05）。总体而言，这些发现表明在临床高危和首发精神病阶段，白质微结构相对稳定，并且与症状严重程度或功能结局随时间的变化没有明确关联。

## Abstract
Psychosis has been linked to changes in diffusion-derived fractional anisotropy (FA) across multiple white matter tracts, yet most evidence is cross-sectional. Longitudinal findings are inconsistent, and the clinical relevance of white matter changes over time remains unclear. To address this gap, we investigated longitudinal white matter changes and their clinical correlates in early psychosis in a large, multi-centre diffusion-tensor imaging study. Across 18 sites, 407 participants (healthy controls: n = 97, 59.8% men, mean age 23.9 years; clinical high risk of psychosis: n = 158, 53.5% men, mean age 23.0 years; first-episode psychosis: n = 152, 71.1% men, mean age 25.1 years) were scanned at up to three time points over 12 months. FA was assessed in the cingulum bundle, the superior longitudinal fasciculus, the inferior fronto-occipital fasciculus, and at the whole brain level. FA trajectories were analysed using linear mixed-effects models to test effects of group, social and occupational functioning, and (attenuated) psychotic symptoms, while controlling for demographic and socioeconomic covariates. No significant group differences in FA were observed, either globally or within tracts (p > .05). Longitudinal FA trajectories were not associated with changes in (attenuated) psychotic symptoms or functioning (p > .05). In contrast, higher baseline antipsychotic medication dose was significantly associated with lower FA (pcorr < .05). Overall, these findings indicate that white matter microstructure is relatively stable during the clinical-high-risk and first-episode phases of psychosis, and that it is not clearly associated with variation in symptom severity or functional outcomes over time.
