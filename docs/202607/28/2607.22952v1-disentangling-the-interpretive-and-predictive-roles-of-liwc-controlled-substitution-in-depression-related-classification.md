## 摘要
语言查询与词频统计（LIWC）提供了可审计的心理语言学类别，广泛用于解释抑郁症相关语言，但其在现代多模态系统中的增量预测作用仍不明确。我们在五个英汉抑郁症相关语料库上进行了匹配参与者级别的交叉验证，评估LIWC是否改善了分类以及任何性能变化所反映的内容。将完整LIWC与三种折叠局部替代版本进行比较：PCA旋转版本（移除了对命名的类别坐标的直接访问）、参与者打乱版本（保留真实的LIWC特征但打破参与者对齐）以及随机边缘版本（保留特征维度的分布）。在多个固定表示上下文中，结果提供了有限的证据表明LIWC在冻结的参与者级别早期融合下具有稳定的增益。所有预先指定的数据集块对比均未通过多重比较校正。单独的SBERT校准产生了更大的完整与打乱以及完整与随机之间的分离，表明在相同程序下，更大的参与者对齐信号可以产生相应更大的分离，但未能解决五个语料库的统计功效限制。LIWC作为可审计的、语料库条件化的解释层仍然有用。这些结论不应推广到微调、序列感知或端到端架构。

## Abstract
Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count (LIWC) provides auditable psycholinguistic categories that are widely used to interpret depression-related language, but its incremental predictive role in modern multimodal systems remains unclear. We evaluate LIWC across five English and Chinese depression-related corpora under matched participant-level cross-validation. We ask whether LIWC improves classification and what any performance change reflects. Intact LIWC is compared with three fold-local substitutes: a PCA-rotated version that removes direct access to named category coordinates, a participant-shuffled version that preserves real LIWC profiles while breaking participant alignment, and a random-marginal version that preserves feature-wise distributions. Across multiple fixed representation contexts, the results provide limited evidence for stable LIWC gains under frozen, participant-level early fusion. None of the prespecified dataset-blocked contrasts survives multiple-comparison correction. A separate SBERT calibration produces larger observed intact-versus-shuffled and intact-versus-random separations, indicating that larger participant-aligned signals can produce correspondingly larger separations under the same procedure, while not resolving the five-corpus power limitation. LIWC remains useful as an auditable, corpus-conditioned interpretive layer. These conclusions should not be generalized to fine-tuned, sequence-aware, or end-to-end architectures.
