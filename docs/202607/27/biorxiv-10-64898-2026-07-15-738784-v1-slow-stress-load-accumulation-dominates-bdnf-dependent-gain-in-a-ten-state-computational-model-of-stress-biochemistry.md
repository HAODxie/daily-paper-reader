## 摘要
背景。急性应激反应通常是可逆的，而持续性应激则可能导致内分泌、代谢、炎症、抗氧化和神经可塑性途径的协调性失调。分级应激生物化学模型（TSBM）是一个产生假设的十状态常微分方程框架，将程式化的皮质醇信号与去甲肾上腺素能驱动、维生素C、现象学上的醛固酮/肾素-血管紧张素驱动、镁、归一化的BDNF相关和Nrf2相关状态、炎症以及色氨酸-犬尿氨酸代谢联系起来。方法。对五种预设情景（正常、急性、慢性、抑郁样和低皮质醇PTSD样）进行了168小时模拟。分析包括局部稳定性、输出特异性灵敏度筛选、结构消融、在独立正负20%参数范围上的400次拉丁超立方扫描、阈值穿越时间的不确定性分布、成功条件参数权衡筛选，以及更广泛的扫描，其中假设的BDNF反馈增益幅度从标称值的0.1倍到10倍呈对数均匀变化。参数被分类为文献来源、文献约束/模型定义、校准或假设。结果。在指定的强迫假设下，持续性应激在多个途径中产生了协调性变化。在抑郁样情景中，去除缓慢的压力负荷积累将第7天的BDNF相关活性从47.2%提高到82.6%，将炎症从14.63任意单位降低到2.15任意单位，并将KYN/TRP从0.173降低到0.057。去除BDNF依赖性增益仅将BDNF提高到49.1%，并将KYN/TRP穿越时间延迟了2.5小时。更严格的多输出结论在91%的不确定性抽取中得以保留。中位穿越时间保留了名义顺序，但完整的四事件顺序仅在所有抽取的43%中出现。结论。在这个简化模型中，一个共享的缓慢压力负荷过程协调了高暴露状态，而BDNF依赖性反馈主要作为放大器。该模型产生了一个可实验验证的分期假说：在持续性高暴露强迫下，镁的变化可能先于后续的BDNF相关和KYN/TRP变化。需要进行纵向研究以确定这种顺序是否在生物学上发生、是否可逆以及是否具有临床相关性。模拟结果不构成诊断或治疗建议。

## Abstract
Background. Acute stress responses are often reversible, whereas sustained stress can produce coordinated disruption across endocrine, metabolic, inflammatory, antioxidant, and neuroplastic pathways. The Tiered Stress Biochemistry Model (TSBM) is a hypothesis-generating ten-state ordinary differential-equation framework linking a stylized cortisol signal to noradrenergic drive, vitamin C, a phenomenological aldosterone/renin-angiotensin drive, magnesium, normalized BDNF-related and Nrf2-related states, inflammation, and tryptophan-kynurenine metabolism. Methods. Five prespecified scenarios (normal, acute, chronic, depression-like, and low-cortisol PTSD-like) were simulated for 168 hours. Analyses included local stability, output-specific sensitivity screening, structural ablation, a 400-draw Latin-hypercube scan over independent plus/minus 20% parameter ranges, uncertainty distributions for threshold-crossing times, a success-conditioned parameter-trade-off screen, and a wider scan in which hypothesized BDNF-feedback gain magnitudes varied log-uniformly from 0.1 to 10 times nominal. Parameters were classified as literature-derived, literature-constrained/model-defined, calibrated, or hypothesized. Results. Under the specified forcing assumptions, sustained stress produced coordinated changes across several pathways. In the depression-like scenario, removing slow stress-load accumulation increased day-7 BDNF-related activity from 47.2% to 82.6%, reduced inflammation from 14.63 to 2.15 arbitrary units, and lowered KYN/TRP from 0.173 to 0.057. Removing BDNF-dependent gain increased BDNF only to 49.1% and delayed KYN/TRP crossing by 2.5 hours. The stricter multi-output conclusion was retained in 91% of uncertainty draws. Median crossing times retained the nominal sequence, but the complete four-event order occurred in only 43% of all draws. Conclusions. Within this reduced model, a shared slow stress-load process coordinates the high-exposure state, whereas BDNF-dependent feedback acts mainly as an amplifier. The model generates an experimentally testable staging hypothesis: under sustained high-exposure forcing, magnesium changes may precede later BDNF-related and KYN/TRP changes. Longitudinal studies are required to determine whether this sequence occurs biologically, whether it is reversible, and whether it has clinical relevance. The simulations are not diagnostic or treatment recommendations.
