## 摘要
帕金森病主要表现为与α-突触核蛋白毒性聚集相关的多巴胺能神经退行性变。京尼平是一种具有生物活性的环烯醚萜类化合物，先前的研究表明，在转基因黑腹果蝇模型系统中，它能改善由泛神经元表达天然α-突触核蛋白引起的运动能力和生存缺陷。我们证明，表达α-突触核蛋白会导致睡眠缺陷，而京尼平处理能挽救这些睡眠缺陷，相对于未处理的表达α-突触核蛋白的果蝇，京尼平增加了总睡眠时间并巩固了夜间睡眠。我们的发现扩展了京尼平在黑腹果蝇中的保护作用，并强调睡眠调节是响应α-突触核蛋白靶向干预的另一个表型。

## Abstract
Parkinson's disease is predominantly characterized by dopaminergic neurodegeneration linked to toxic aggregation of -synuclein. Genipin, a bioactive iridoid, was previously shown to improve the motility and survival deficits caused by pan-neuronal expression of native -synuclein in a transgenic Drosophila melanogaster model system. We show that expression of -synuclein causes sleep deficits and that genipin treatment rescued these sleep deficits, increasing total sleep and consolidating nighttime sleep relative to untreated -synuclein--xpressing fruit flies. Our findings extend genipin'ss protective profile in Drosophila melanogaster and highlight sleep regulation as an additional phenotype responsive to -synuclein--targeted interventions.
