## 摘要
特质焦虑是常见的术前表型，并与术后不良认知结局相关，但尚未在将麻醉与手术损伤分离的受控设计中测试其是否调节对全身麻醉的反应。我们通过对线索恐惧记忆提取僵直进行无监督k-medoids聚类，将42只雄性C57BL/6N小鼠分为低焦虑（LA，n=24）或高焦虑（HA，n=18）组，并在未进行手术的情况下，在实时脑电图滴定下使所有动物暴露于七氟烷。在足够的麻醉深度下，HA小鼠的非周期性（1/f）皮质斜率比LA小鼠更平坦（AUC=0.85，[0.70, 0.95]）。常规水十字迷宫评分在麻醉后复测时结果为阴性，但同一试验视频的无监督分解将效应定位为HA小鼠特定学习转换的丧失（AUC=0.72，[0.56, 0.86]）。暗期快速眼动睡眠反应方向反转，HA小鼠相对于自身基线失去快速眼动睡眠，而LA小鼠则获得（AUC=0.82，[0.65, 0.94]）。这三种效应在方向上与七氟烷下皮质兴奋-抑制平衡的调节一致。可从常规额叶脑电图恢复的非周期性指数和苏醒期脑电图轨迹是候选的麻醉前生物标志物。

## Abstract
Trait anxiety is a common pre-surgical phenotype and has been linked to adverse postoperative cognitive outcome, but whether it modulates the response to general anaesthesia has not been tested in a controlled design that isolates the anaesthetic from surgical injury. We classified 42 male C57BL/6N mice as low-anxiety (LA, n = 24) or high-anxiety (HA, n = 18) by unsupervised k-medoids clustering on cued fear-retrieval freezing, and exposed all animals to sevoflurane under live-EEG titration without surgery. At adequate anaesthetic depth, HA mice carried a flatter aperiodic (1/f) cortical slope than LA mice (AUC = 0.85, [0.70, 0.95]). Conventional water-cross-maze scoring returned null at post-anaesthetic re-test, but unsupervised decomposition of the same trial videos localised the effect to loss of a specific learned transition in HA mice (AUC = 0.72, [0.56, 0.86]). The dark-phase REMS response reversed direction, with HA mice losing REMS against their own baseline while LA mice gained it (AUC = 0.82, [0.65, 0.94]). The three effects are directionally consistent with modulation of cortical excitation-to-inhibition balance under sevoflurane. The aperiodic exponent and emergence-phase EEG trajectory, both recoverable from routine frontal EEG, are candidate pre-anaesthetic biomarkers.
