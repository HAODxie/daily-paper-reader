## 摘要
去甲肾上腺素能蓝斑及其神经调节性皮层投射对于适应性行为至关重要，然而由于无创评估该系统的挑战，它们在人类新环境内隐学习中的作用尚未得到充分理解。本研究结合多模态神经影像学——包括蓝斑敏感的结构MRI、同步瞳孔测量-EEG、瞳孔测量-fMRI以及PET衍生的去甲肾上腺素转运体图谱——与重复行为评估，以探究年轻人与老年人（n=77）中去甲肾上腺素能对内隐学习的贡献。违反预期的显著刺激诱发了瞳孔扩张，提示神经调节增强，激活了行动模式网络并默认模式网络失活。与瞳孔相关的BOLD反应表明蓝斑与行动模式网络之间存在功能耦合，这一发现进一步得到激活模式与PET衍生去甲肾上腺素转运体图谱空间重叠的支持。蓝斑MRI引导的功能连接分析显示蓝斑与前岛叶活动耦合，提示去甲肾上腺素能在将皮层动态转向行动导向处理中发挥作用。行为上，参与者逐渐内隐学习了统计任务结构，根据刺激概率调整反应时间。关键的是，更强的蓝斑完整性、更大的任务相关前岛叶激活以及更明显的瞳孔扩张与增强的内隐学习相关联，突显了去甲肾上腺素能神经调节的行为相关性。值得注意的是，去甲肾上腺素能反应及其与学习的联系在不同年龄组中得以保持，表明去甲肾上腺素能在整个成年期支持适应性行为中具有稳健作用。互补的电生理分析揭示了瞳孔相关的皮层兴奋性增加，这伴随着从默认模式网络向行动模式网络激活的转变。总之，这些发现为学习和认知灵活性背后的神经调节机制提供了新见解，强调了蓝斑-行动模式网络相互作用在行为适应中的关键作用。

## Abstract
The noradrenergic locus coeruleus and its neuromodulatory cortical projections are critical for adaptive behavior, however how they contribute to human implicit learning in novel environments is not yet well understood, due to the challenges of assessing this system non-invasively. Here, we combined multimodal neuroimaging--including locus-coeruleus-sensitive structural MRI, concurrent pupillometry-EEG, pupillometry-fMRI, and PET-derived noradrenergic transporter maps--with repeated behavioral assessments to investigate noradrenergic contributions to implicit learning across younger and older adults (n = 77). Salient expectation-violating stimuli elicited pupil dilation, indicating enhanced neuromodulation, activated the action-mode network and deactivated the default-mode network. Pupil-linked BOLD responses suggested a functional coupling between the locus coeruleus and action-mode network, further supported by spatial overlap of activation patterns with PET-derived noradrenergic transporter maps. Locus coeruleus MRI-guided functional connectivity analyses demonstrated coupled locus coeruleus and anterior insula activity, suggesting a noradrenergic role in shifting cortical dynamics toward action-oriented processing. Behaviorally, participants implicitly learned the statistical task structure over time, shifting their response times based on stimulus probabilities. Critically, stronger locus coeruleus integrity, greater task-related anterior insula activation, and more pronounced pupil dilation were associated with enhanced implicit learning, highlighting the behavioral relevance of noradrenergic neuromodulation. Notably, noradrenergic responses and their link to learning were preserved across age groups, suggesting a robust noradrenergic role in supporting adaptive behavior throughout adulthood. Complementary electrophysiological analyses revealed pupil-linked increases in cortical excitability that were coupled with shifts from default-mode to action-mode network activation. Together, these findings provide novel insights into the neuromodulatory mechanisms underlying learning and cognitive flexibility, emphasizing the pivotal role of locus coeruleus-action-mode network interactions in behavioral adaptation.
