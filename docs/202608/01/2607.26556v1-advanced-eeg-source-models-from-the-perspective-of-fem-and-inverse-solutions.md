## 摘要
在本研究中，我们比较了使用Zeffiro Interface和DUNEuro中的有限元方法计算的正向解，采用了它们提供的不同源模型。我们比较了DUNEuro中的两种源模型，即Whitney基和局部减法，以及Zeffiro Interface的散度相容模型。对于源估计，我们应用了促进稀疏性的标准化分层自适应L1回归（SHAL1R）、标准化卡尔曼滤波（SKF）、经典sLORETA和偶极子扫描。分析包括推土机距离、深度偏差散点图，以及幅度分布和聚焦性的定性评估。对每种方法进行源插值的初步实验表明，局部减法与导联场在不同深度的局部行为预期非常吻合。主要结果表明，逆方法能否成功很大程度上取决于其对源聚焦性的假设与所选源模型之间的兼容性，点源模型在与针对此类源设计的方法（即促进稀疏性的方法和扫描单个源的方法）配对时表现最佳。此外，允许具有广泛分布的片状源的源模型对额外噪声更敏感。

## Abstract
In this study, we compare forward solutions computed with finite element methods in Zeffiro Interface and DUNEuro, using the different source models they provided. We compared two of the source models from DUNEuro, called Whitney basis and Local subtraction, and the divergence-conforming model of Zeffiro Interface. For source estimation, we applied sparsity-promoting standardized hierarchical adaptive L1 regression (SHAL1R), standardized Kalman filtering (SKF), classical sLORETA, and dipole scanning. Analyses include Earth Mover's Distance, depth bias scatter plots, and qualitative assessments of amplitude distribution and focality. Preliminary experiments with source interpolation for each method revealed that Local subtraction closely matches expectations for the local behavior of the lead field at various depths. The main results reveal that the success of an inverse method depends strongly on the compatibility between its assumptions about the focality of the source and the chosen source model, with point-source models performing best when paired with methods designed for such sources, i.e., sparsity-promoting methods and methods that scan for a single source. Moreover, source models that admit patch sources with a wide distribution are more sensitive to additional noise.
